## Continuity of evidence

The project is not starting over. All prior experiments, negative results, commits,
preregistrations, classifications, and artifacts remain valid historical development
evidence and immutable audit history. This rebaseline changes how **future** evidence is
generated and interpreted; it does not reset or rescue any strategy.

The former default loop—new source, new candidate, historical test, another source—is
stopped. Future research separates four questions:

1. statistical credibility of the evidence;
2. informational content of a signal;
3. economic monetization and execution;
4. product validation.

A positive development backtest is not sufficient for product admission. Statistical
credibility, matched-control comparison, economic significance, realistic execution,
and the applicable evidence hierarchy must all be governed explicitly.

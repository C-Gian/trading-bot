# Executor policy

Codex is the primary high-throughput engineering executor. Astra Ultra is the
executor for scientific, architecture, red-team, and algorithm-design work where
reasoning quality materially matters. Do not avoid Ultra solely to preserve usage.
These are task-allocation roles, not dependencies of the product architecture.

The Research Director owns research direction within Owner-controlled governance.
Scientific truth lives in versioned repository records and deterministic artifacts,
not agent conversations. No agent can authorize real capital.

WP-004 executes one bounded family, at most three structural variants, after
committed design and preregistration. Historical development findings remain
adaptive development evidence even when arranged into chronological folds.

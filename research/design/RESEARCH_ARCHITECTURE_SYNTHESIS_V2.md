## 2 — The resolvability problem this synthesis must answer

The recurring blocker across P1A, P2 and the challenger families is not that BTC lacks
plausible mechanisms. It is that a single asset over a fixed development window supplies
too little independent information to resolve economically meaningful effect sizes at
the declared alpha and power. Adding another BTC-only source or another BTC-only model
does not change that denominator.

## 3 — Candidate research directions

Exactly three directions are compared. None is executed here.

### A — BTC-only new signal / mechanism research

- **Economic usefulness.** Directly aimed at the product. A working BTC signal is
  immediately usable by BTCUSDT spot V1.
- **Statistical resolvability.** Poor, and the binding constraint. The same clustered,
  finite BTC development sample that blocked P1A and cost-dominated every challenger
  applies unchanged.
- **New degrees of freedom.** High. Each new source or model class opens a fresh search
  family and enlarges the known discovery family beyond the current 12.
- **Implementation / data burden.** Moderate: one more point-in-time source or model.
- **Relation to BTCUSDT V1.** Direct.
- **Risk of repeating a failed family.** High. Internal, macro, regime, contextual,
  shallow-nonlinear, funding, attention and CFTC families have already been searched
  along this axis with no robust positive post-cost result.

### B — Cross-sectional replication / common-effect research across crypto

- **Economic usefulness.** Indirect but material. It tests whether one already-observed
  mechanism is a general economic effect rather than a BTC-specific artifact, which is
  the evidence a promotion decision on BTC actually needs.
- **Statistical resolvability.** The best of the three. A pooled primary endpoint over
  many assets brings substantially more information to bear on **one** common hypothesis,
  provided cross-asset and cross-time dependence is modelled honestly rather than by
  pretending assets are independent replicates.
- **New degrees of freedom.** Moderate and controllable, but genuinely dangerous if
  mishandled: universe construction, eligibility and per-asset selection are all new
  choice surfaces that must be frozen before exposure.
- **Implementation / data burden.** The highest of the three: point-in-time universe,
  listing and delisting history, survivorship controls, liquidity eligibility, per-asset
  cost models.
- **Relation to BTCUSDT V1.** Generalization evidence only. The product is unchanged.
- **Risk of repeating a failed family.** Low. No cross-sectional family has been opened.

### C — Prospective evidence collection / product-side evidence accumulation

- **Economic usefulness.** High in the long run and the only path to the strongest
  evidence class the constitution recognises, but it yields nothing quickly.
- **Statistical resolvability.** Currently zero and improving only with wall-clock time:
  0 genuine completed paper trades so far.
- **New degrees of freedom.** None. It adds no search burden.
- **Implementation / data burden.** Already built and available.
- **Relation to BTCUSDT V1.** It *is* the product path.
- **Risk of repeating a failed family.** None.

## 4 — Allocation

**Primary next research direction: `CROSS_SECTIONAL_FEASIBILITY_AND_POWER_DESIGN`.**

Reason: BTC-only ALIGNED persistence is severely power-limited, while a properly frozen
cross-sectional study can test ONE common economic mechanism using more information
without treating each asset as an independent hypothesis.

This does **not** mean any of the following:

- ALIGNED is validated;
- BTC is validated;
- the product becomes multi-asset;
- 40 assets become 40 hypotheses;
- new assets may be cherry-picked.

Product remains **BTCUSDT spot V1**. The cross-section is initially a scientific
generalization / power investigation only.

**Secondary parallel direction: `PROSPECTIVE_PAPER_EVIDENCE_CONTINUES`.** Paper strategy
semantics are unchanged by this synthesis. It runs in parallel because it costs no
degrees of freedom and accumulates the strongest evidence class available.

**Cycle research: `PARKED_METHODOLOGY_BLOCKED`.** No Null V3 is authorized. No alternate
block length, period band, representation, GARCH/HAR/FIGARCH model, bootstrap or
spectral estimator may be tried as part of P2 V1. Reopening cycle research requires a
new structural hypothesis and protocol version after a future Research Director
allocation.

**BTC-only new source/model search: `DEPRIORITIZED`.** New alternative data, new ML
architectures, new threshold optimization and another cycle-null method stay closed
without a later explicit Research Director allocation.

# System G1 Playbook P2 — Failed-Auction Re-entry

Status: **REGISTERED — HISTORICAL OUTCOME EXECUTION NOT AUTHORIZED**
Generation: SYSTEM_G1

## Proposition

In a bounded market, an attempt to trade beyond a boundary that was already known before the event
may fail. A causal re-entry into the prior range can define a mean-reverting trade toward a
pre-existing value reference.

Lower-boundary failure may propose LONG.
Upper-boundary failure may propose SHORT.

The setup is the **failure and re-entry**, not the initial breakout and not a future-labelled turn.

## Required roles

- bounded/RANGE market state;
- previous completed UTC day's high/low as pre-known boundary;
- completed 15m excursion and re-entry trigger;
- value/VWAP reference known at trigger;
- participation/aggressive-flow family as fixed corroboration if admitted by G1;
- cycle family as timing qualifier only after method readiness;
- volatility/risk normalization;
- data/execution quality veto.

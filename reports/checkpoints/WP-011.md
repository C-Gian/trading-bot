## What was and was not tested

WP-008 falsified a fixed global linear combination of eight internal features. It never
tested time-varying weights, macro information, or recency weighting. WP-011 tests
exactly those three and nothing else. This is a single rejection, not a verdict on the
Owner's broader multi-signal thesis.

## Model

`EXPONENTIALLY_WEIGHTED_LINEAR_NET_R_V1`: weighted least squares with intercept, one
model effective at the first UTC hour of each calendar month and held fixed until the
next. Recency weight `exp(-ln(2) * age_days / 180)` against the effective instant;
training-only weighted mean and population variance under the same weights. A row enters
training only when its signal instant *and* complete label outcome both precede
`effective - 216h`. The 180-day half-life was frozen prospectively; no alternative was
evaluated, and there was no regularization, threshold search, feature dropping,
coefficient thresholding or validation refit.

One pre-execution correction was recorded before any market result: the first monthly
model (2019-01) is inadmissible because `UNRATE_LEVEL` has weighted standard deviation
4.44e-16 over its 1,314-row training window. The frozen hard fail stands — the fit is
refused and recorded, the hours it would have covered emit nothing, and no feature,
half-life, threshold or gate was changed. 144 fits were reserved; 143 were admissible.

## Did macro help?

No — it made things materially worse. Adding the eight point-in-time macro features cost
**-0.0460 R per trade** (-0.0988 vs -0.0527), took fold stability from 2 of 6 to 0 of 6,
worsened cost robustness, and barely moved the correlation (+0.0275 vs +0.0243) while
almost doubling the trade count. The macro block absorbed **69.7%** of mean absolute
standardized coefficient mass (41.2%–89.4%), crowding out market price (16.4%),
volatility/liquidity (8.4%) and order flow (5.5%). That weight bought no predictive
content.

Macro coefficient signs are not stable across the 71 monthly fits — `T10Y2Y_LEVEL` 0.61,
`NFCI_LEVEL` 0.61, `WALCL_LOG_CHANGE_28D` 0.55, `DFF_LEVEL` 0.65 — which is the profile
of fitting noise rather than a durable economic relationship. The most sign-stable term
is `TAKER_BUY_SHARE_4H_CENTERED` at 0.93, carrying about 5% of the mass.

## Did time-varying weighting beat the fixed architecture?

Marginally, and not enough to matter. WP-008 `LINEAR_FULL` scored -0.1142 R on 1,003
trades; the adaptive primary -0.0988 R on 1,487. Both are `REJECT_COST_DOMINATED` and the
trade sets are not paired. The adaptive internal-only configuration is the least bad of
the three, suggesting recency weighting helped slightly while macro hurt more — but it is
still negative.

Historical comparisons against no-trade, random, trend, breakout, ALIGNED, pullback,
order-flow and WP-008 are recorded descriptively only; eligibility universes and trade
counts differ and nothing is presented as paired.

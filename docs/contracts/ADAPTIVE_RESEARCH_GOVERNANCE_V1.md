# Adaptive research governance V1

Search ledger admissions and immutable outcomes are the authoritative record of
what was tried. The family budget is a cumulative envelope, never a per-name reset.
New allocations require a committed Research Director decision citing prior usage,
the added units, and the scientific basis; this does not grant sealed queries.

## Evidence and accounting units

| Kind | Meaning | Accounting |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed preregistered trial | One frozen rule/configuration | Experiment, configuration, execution profile |
| Bounded family exploration | Several structural tests of one economic claim | Every configuration and descendant; one shared core hypothesis |
| Adaptive follow-up | A design choice motivated by observed results | Decision ID, triggering result hashes, fork/parent IDs |
| Robustness test | Predeclared cost/timing/ablation diagnostic | Execution profile; never an independent discovery |
| Development walk-forward | Frozen chronological validation components | Fold executions tracked; no extra independent hypothesis credit |
| Sealed evaluation | Controlled unexposed data query | Separate explicit query budget; zero in WP-004 |
| Future paper evidence | Immutable prospective observations | Separate prospective count; zero in WP-004 |

WP-003 spent six experiment IDs, six configurations, and 41 execution profiles
(32 random seeds, four cost stresses, and five other default/reference/control
profiles). Its two economic core hypotheses were trend and breakout. Controls and
the endpoint reference remain separately visible. None of the 32 random seeds is
an independent claim of market mechanism.

WP-004 allocates one additional core hypothesis, three structural configurations,
12 execution profiles (three defaults plus nine cost/timing diagnostics), and one
adaptive decision/fork from observed WP-003 results. Six fixed annual components
per profile produce 72 fold executions; they do not make 72 independent trials.
The cumulative envelope is nine experiments, nine configurations, 53 profiles,
three economic core hypotheses. The breakout root retains all four experiments
and all four configurations including its original negative baseline.

No fold outcome changes a rule, threshold, schedule, or which configuration is
primary. No optimizer or optional stopping exists. A failed execution consumes
its attempted allocation and is preserved; an evidence-affecting correction
requires a new version and explicit allocation, never overwriting a result.
Mechanical corrections that precede any market output are recorded in Git.

## What development evidence can claim

All 2017–2024 history has already contributed baseline summaries. New folds are
chronological development validation, not freshly unexposed out-of-sample evidence.
Preregistration limits future adaptation but does not erase that exposure.
Reporting must carry cumulative search counts and lineage alongside any favorable
result. Do not attach naive independent-trial p-values or claim that a best
configuration is the first test. Stability, count, and ESS diagnostics are
descriptive safeguards, not multiple-testing correction or calibrated confidence.

Family interpretation follows the predeclared ALIGNED primary rule. A favorable
ablation cannot silently replace it. Any further allocation must explicitly
acknowledge that result-dependent choice. Structural validator PASS depends on
integrity and complete accounting, never profitability.

Enforcement combines schemas, deterministic admission, append-only Git chronology,
complete dependency/config hashes, exact runner allowlists, and result linkage.
Arbitrary code or dishonest mechanism metadata can evade semantic descriptions;
no file-based validator proves absence of secret off-repository experiments.
Independent review and custody boundaries remain necessary over a multi-year program.

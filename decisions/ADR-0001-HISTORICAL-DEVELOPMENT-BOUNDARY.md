# ADR-0001: Historical development boundary

Status: Accepted (before data acquisition)

BTCUSDT Binance Spot is the sole initial asset. Canonical development data is 1m and may end no later than `2024-12-31T23:59:00Z`. Detailed later bars are reserved and must not be fetched, inspected, charted, summarized, or used by research agents. The reserved interval is intended for controlled evaluation but is not sealed evidence until isolation exists. Prospective paper evidence remains stronger. Disappointing results cannot move the cutoff later; any change requires a new scientific ADR before affected data is viewed.

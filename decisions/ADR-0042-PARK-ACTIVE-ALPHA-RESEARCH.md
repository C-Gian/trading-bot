## Scientific interpretation

Parking is a resource-allocation conclusion, not an impossibility claim.

The programme has not proven that BTC contains no predictable or tradeable behaviour, nor that every
professional trading mechanism fails. It has established that, under the current BTC spot
LONG/NO_TRADE, public-information, execution and <=12-month confirmation envelope, no currently
identified candidate has earned a practical, reproducible trading claim and no remaining reserve
mechanism has enough expected information value to justify another allocation.

Candidate #1 materially strengthens the stop decision because it was selected after the strategic
reset, passed source/support admission, used a frozen mechanism-led playbook with a matched control,
and still failed absolute economics, incremental economics, robustness, risk and prospective
detectability.

## Operational meaning

While parked:

- active alpha discovery and promotion work stops;
- no new Candidate Cards are created;
- no old lineage is rescued through thresholds, horizons, costs, filters, subgroups or models;
- no alpha-driven data acquisition or prospective collection begins;
- existing evidence, code, data, manifests, protocols and replay instructions are preserved;
- action surfaces must not imply a validated trading strategy;
- `NO_TRADE` is the honest operational action output because no approved opportunity process exists.

The repository may perform one finite archival/reconciliation work package to make this parked state
unambiguous and reproducible. That work may not access market outcomes, acquire data or create new
research questions.

## Reopening triggers

Parking may be reviewed only when a concrete dossier establishes a material changed case, such as:

1. genuinely new accessible information with usable causal timing/provenance and a specific distinct
   mechanism;
2. a structural execution-cost/access change that removes a material constraint **and** leaves a
   surviving economic proposition;
3. an identifiable material market-structure change affecting participant constraints or
   information transmission;
4. compelling independent evidence directly relevant to an implementable mechanism under realistic
   timing/cost/dependence conditions;
5. an Owner-approved material product/risk/resource change.

A new indicator, new AI model, more elapsed time, renewed enthusiasm, a different price regime or the
fact that some concept remains untested is not a reopening trigger.

Every reopening requires Astra strategic approval after the dossier is written. A material
product/risk/resource change may additionally require explicit Owner approval. Real capital always
requires a separate explicit Owner decision.

## Non-alpha product value

Existing engineering assets may have value as a market-state monitor, educational/research tool or
paper-research laboratory. This ADR does not authorize that pivot. Changing Trading Bot's product
objective away from the trading-system objective requires an Owner decision.

Its purposes are to:

Owner-approved material objective change.

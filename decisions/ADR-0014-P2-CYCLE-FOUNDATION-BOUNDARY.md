# ADR-0014 — P1A power block and P2 time-only cycle foundation

Status: accepted as research direction and structural design. No market result was
executed or observed.

## Context

P1A's immutable prospective gate reports `P1A_INFORMATION_MESI_BPS = 24.0`, empirical
MDE 285.640052 bps/event, power 0.01424888 at MESI, and
`POWER_GATE_STATUS = REDESIGN_REQUIRED`. The true zero-shift ALIGNED statistic remains
uncomputed. Treating that gate as market rejection would confuse inability to resolve a
claim with evidence against the claim.

The research architecture also needs to avoid rewarding sparse selectivity when the
available evidence cannot resolve the proposed effect, and cycle research needs a hard
boundary between public concepts and project-reconstructed rules.

## Decision

1. Disposition `ALIGNED_SIGNAL_PERSISTS_TO_120H_V1` as
   `POWER_BLOCKED_NOT_EXECUTED`. This is neither `REJECT` nor `INCONCLUSIVE` market
   evidence, consumes no observed material hypothesis, and authorizes no ALIGNED rescue,
   tuning, alternative horizon, or asset expansion.
2. Admit the two-axis architecture in
   `research/design/RESEARCH_ARCHITECTURE_SYNTHESIS_V1.md`: economic usefulness and
   statistical resolvability are both mandatory before execution. Architecture slots
   are not approved components, ALIGNED is not the final architecture, and weights are
   not optimized.
3. Bind all Ciclica Evoluta / Analisi Evoluta attribution to
   `docs/canonical/CYCLE_RESEARCH_SOURCE_BOUNDARY_V1.md`. Project-invented executable
   detail is `RECONSTRUCTED`; absent universal public rules remain `UNSPECIFIED`.
4. Begin P2 with exactly one structural, non-economic, time-only hypothesis:
   `BTC_TIME_CYCLE_STRUCTURE_V1`. Freeze at most two non-rescuing diagnostics and a
   prospective dependent-null injection/power design before exposing the actual result.
5. Keep selection inside chronological training and evaluation outside it. Recreate
   frequency selection in every null and synthetic replicate. Do not assign an economic
   MESI to this structural test.
6. If a future cycle result motivates a trading rule, require a new
   `MATERIAL_ECONOMIC_HYPOTHESIS` with its own preregistration, MESI, power gate, costs,
   execution, and search accounting.

## Consequences

P2 has one primary and a maximum of two diagnostics, but zero executed economic
hypotheses. No cycle implementation or market outcome exists yet. Swing, volume,
inverse, vincolo, raccordo, target, and cycle-based trading stay closed. The next work
package is `P2-CYCLE-FOUNDATION-POWER-GATE-PREP`; after P2 is resolved,
`RESEARCH-ARCHITECTURE-SYNTHESIS-V2` is mandatory before another hypothesis family opens.

Scientific counters remain: 26 completed experiments, 12 observed material economic
hypotheses, zero sealed queries, Champion NONE, and real money false.

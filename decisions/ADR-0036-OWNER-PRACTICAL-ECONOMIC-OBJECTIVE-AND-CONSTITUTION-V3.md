# ADR-0036 — Practical economic usefulness objective, Constitution Version 3.0 and Astra hierarchy

Status: ACCEPTED (Owner-authorized, 2026-09-25)

Material Owner-controlled change of product objective and research governance. It supersedes
the prediction-first mission of
[ADR-0026](ADR-0026-PREDICTION-FIRST-RESEARCH-OBJECTIVE.md) as the current mission. No prior
scientific result is altered, reinterpreted or deleted.

## Authority

- Owner clarification of 25 September 2026: Trading Bot is intended to become a practical,
  reproducible BTC spot `LONG` / `NO_TRADE` system; professional trading mechanisms generate
  bounded hypotheses and statistics evaluates them.
- `ASTRA_TRADING_BOT_STRATEGIC_OPERATING_DIRECTIVE_V2` (25 September 2026) — current strategic
  authority; V1 is historical context.
- The historical evidence-salvage audit (Candidate #1 lineage adjudication).
- Work package `GOVERNANCE-TRANSITION-AND-CANDIDATE-ADMISSION-V1`
  (`tasks/archive/GOVERNANCE-TRANSITION-AND-CANDIDATE-ADMISSION-V1.md`), which records the
  Owner's authorization to produce the next Constitution version.

## Context

Constitution Version 2.0 made a statistically credible predictor the mission and staged
information families through a universal source ladder. Two prediction-first generations ran
under it; every tested family was rejected, blocked or power-blocked, and the latest (1h
incremental taker flow) was power-blocked without execution
([ADR-0035](ADR-0035-PUBLIC-TAKER-FLOW-1H-INCREMENTAL-POWER-BLOCK.md)). Continuing to invent
ever more elaborate predictive questions after each failure is not the Owner's product
objective, and exposed historical development kept being treated as if rescoring could make it
fresh.

## Decision

1. **Constitution Version 3.0 — practical economic usefulness.** The final product
   advancement objective is practical, risk-constrained economic usefulness demonstrated by
   frozen prospective economic confirmation. Prediction quality remains a distinct scientific
   quantity where predictions are used. `NO_TRADE` is a valid operational output and a valid
   permanent project outcome. Version 2.0 is preserved verbatim as Appendix B; Version 1.0
   remains verbatim as Appendix A. Every leakage, causal-timing, preregistration,
   negative-memory, search-accounting, dependence, sealed-data and real-capital protection is
   carried forward unweakened; the evidence hierarchy is preserved and prospective evidence
   outranks exposed historical development.
2. **Research sequence.**
   `PLAUSIBLE MECHANISM -> EXPLICIT PLAYBOOK -> BOUNDED HISTORICAL DEVELOPMENT -> PROMOTION GATE -> FROZEN PROSPECTIVE ECONOMIC CONFIRMATION`,
   defined in `docs/canonical/RESEARCH_STAGE_POLICY_V1.md` (Development Lab, Promotion Gate,
   Confirmation, Frontier R&D, Astra escalation). Confirmation defaults: power 0.80 at the
   economic MESI, family-wise alpha 0.05, 12-month calendar ceiling. A p-value alone is never
   promotion. The universal source ladder no longer authorizes work; information families enter
   only as inputs to an admitted mechanism.
3. **Decision hierarchy.** Astra is the strategic scientific authority for material
   research-allocation decisions; the ChatGPT Research Director owns routine scientific and
   technical decisions inside Astra's directive; Claude Code is the sole coding and repository
   implementation executor. Codex is not part of the normal implementation workflow.
   `governance/EXECUTOR_POLICY.md`, `AGENTS.md` and `docs/operations/NEW_CHAT_BOOTSTRAP.md` are
   reconciled accordingly.
4. **Strategic allocation map** `docs/canonical/STRATEGIC_ALLOCATION_MAP_V1.md`, with the
   dispositions of the Astra V2 directive. The prior Astra proposal
   `90-day paired-noise pilot -> <=12-month incremental-flow confirmation` is withdrawn and
   receives zero further allocation. Exactly one Candidate Card is allocated (#1); Candidate
   Card #2 is not.
5. **Append-only methodological qualifications** `reports/reviews/METHODOLOGICAL-QUALIFICATIONS-V1.md`:
   the incremental power-gate sign-description mismatch, the invalid "look-ahead can only
   flatter" macro reasoning of ADR-0028, and the composition dependence of the V2 pooled
   enrichment. No rerun, rewrite or rescue.
6. **Programme descriptors.** Allocation posture `LATE_STAGE_WITHIN_CURRENT_ENVELOPE` /
   `RESEARCH_WARNING`; scientific coverage
   `MATURE_NEGATIVE_PREDICTOR_RESEARCH_WITH_LIMITED_PLAYBOOK_COVERAGE`. Neither expands the
   budget.

## Consequences

- Historical generations (`COST_EXPECTANCY_RESEARCH_GENERATION_V1`,
  `PREDICTIVE_RESEARCH_GENERATION_V1`, `PREDICTIVE_RESEARCH_GENERATION_V2`) keep every result
  and disposition; state records that describe them are not rewritten.
- The legacy `project_phase` / `primary_research_phase` labels in state are historical labels;
  current strategic truth is `state.governance_transition_v3`.
- No market trial is authorized by this decision. Champion `NONE`, sealed queries 0, real money
  false.

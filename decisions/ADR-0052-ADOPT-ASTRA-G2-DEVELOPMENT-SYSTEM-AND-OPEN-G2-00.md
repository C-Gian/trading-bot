## Decision

Adopt Astra's strategic allocation of `G2_DEVELOPMENT_SYSTEM` and open only
`G2-00-KNOWLEDGE-TO-CONTRACTS-V1`.

This is a bounded successor development programme. It is not a validated strategy, not a G1 rescue
and not an authorization to inspect protected market outcomes.

## G1 remains terminal

G1 remains `SYSTEM_G1_DEVELOPMENT_REJECTED_SELECTION_STAGE` and
`SYSTEM_G1_TERMINAL_PARK`.

No Phase B, threshold rescue, exit rescue, component rescue or relabelled G1 descendant is
authorized. Reusable infrastructure may survive only with explicit lineage and without carrying
forward G1's rejected economic claim.

## Reopening condition

ADR-0050 required a material changed case plus Astra approval before allocating a successor. That
condition is satisfied by Owner Product Mission V2, the complete accessible professional-trading
knowledge programme, the explicit change in development/evaluation governance and Astra's G2
directive.

The successor is materially different in scientific object and workflow: continuous forecast,
separate policy/actionability, separate risk/execution, bounded transparent predictive readouts,
full prediction/trade autopsy and an exposed development sandbox whose adaptation is accounted for
rather than mislabeled as confirmation.

## Constitution compatibility

No Constitution change is made.

The current Constitution already permits bounded historical development, preregistered trial
budgets, exposed-development evidence, stronger protected/prospective confirmation and Astra
allocation of material research generations.

The G1-specific one-batch sequence remains historical truth for G1. Any future proposal that weakens
the Owner-controlled evidence hierarchy, protected-data boundary or real-capital gate requires the
appropriate Owner decision.

## Design constants are not evidence

Astra's numerical conventions for clocks, refits, model bounds, trade geometry, risk and development
budget are G2 design decisions, not empirical truths imported from the library.

G2-00 must encode them before any economic replay. After outcomes are observed, changing an
economically material convention is a scientific revision under the G2 ledger and cannot be called
an engineering fix merely to avoid the research budget.

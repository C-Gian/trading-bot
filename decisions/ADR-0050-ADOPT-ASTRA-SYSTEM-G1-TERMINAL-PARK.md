## Scientific interpretation

G1 demonstrated an unsuccessful frozen system but did not identify a sufficiently specific,
independently justified architectural correction for a new system generation.

This is not an impossibility claim about professional trading, BTC, cycles, pullbacks, failed
auctions, trend, volume or multi-signal reasoning.

The current conclusion is an allocation decision:

under the present evidence/resource envelope, further strategy/system search is not justified.

## Permanent no-rescue boundary

The exact G1 seven-configuration batch and its post-result descendants are closed.

Do not rehabilitate G1 by:

- threshold or indicator-period drift;
- favorable component removals;
- alternate stop/target multiples;
- altered cycle corroboration;
- LONG-only/SHORT-only selection;
- risk-stop reset/removal;
- forecast-entry integration chosen because G1 failed;
- weighted-score or model-fit replacement chosen because G1 was sparse/loss-making.

A genuinely distinct future system requires a new Astra allocation after a valid reopening trigger.

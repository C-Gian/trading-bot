# ADR-0022 — ALIGNED final historical closure and prospective shadow observer

Status: ACCEPTED. Date: 2026-09-16. Checkpoint:
`ALIGNED-DEVELOPMENT-FINAL-CLOSURE + PROSPECTIVE-SHADOW-PAPER-OBSERVER-V1`.

## Historical closure

The Research Director accepts the final historical status
`PARKED_DEVELOPMENT_SEARCH_EXHAUSTED`. The gate-intensity causal-panel correction passed,
but the byte-frozen calendar-randomization family accepted zero of 1,024 vectors. The
workflow therefore stopped before empirical power or any real coefficient. Neither the
gate-intensity beta nor the retired sparse cross-sectional beta was observed.

This is closure of further historical ALIGNED descendant search, not a rejection of
ALIGNED market performance. Gate weighting, 2-of-3, individual-gate tests, alternative
horizons/placebos/thresholds, and cross-sectional rescue remain unauthorized without a
new explicit Research Director allocation. All historical artifacts remain immutable.

## Research allocation

`PRIMARY_RESEARCH_PHASE = PROSPECTIVE_EVIDENCE_COLLECTION` and
`HISTORICAL_DISCOVERY_STATUS = PAUSED`. ALIGNED remains the unchanged
`PAPER_RESEARCH_CANDIDATE` for BTCUSDT spot V1, LONG/NO_TRADE, paper only. Champion is
`NONE`; real money remains false.

## Separate automated evidence class

Create `FUTURE_SHADOW_PAPER_EVIDENCE_V1` with evidence stage
`AUTOMATED_PROSPECTIVE_SHADOW_PAPER` and initiation mode
`AUTOMATED_RESEARCH_OBSERVER`. Its ledger and operational health stores are physically
separate from manual `FUTURE_PAPER_EVIDENCE_V2`; manual behavior and evidence remain
unchanged.

When the local backend starts, one durable observer activates. Its first eligible UTC
hour boundary is strictly after activation. It never catches up missed signals. A sound
decision must be durable within five minutes or the boundary permanently becomes
`MISSED_PROSPECTIVE_DECISION`. NO_TRADE and LONG observations both remain evidence.

A LONG first commits an unarmed durable shadow intent. Entry can use only the first
available completed BTCUSDT one-minute open strictly after that persistence. One active
shadow intent/position is allowed; another LONG is retained as
`LONG_SIGNAL_SUPPRESSED_ACTIVE_SHADOW_POSITION` without opening a trade.

Already-entered prospective trades may reconcile after restart because their intent,
entry, and rule were durable before downtime bars existed. Pending entries and missed
signals may not be reconstructed. The frozen 2% stop, 4% target, 1,440-minute horizon,
`STOP_FIRST_V1`, and `BTCUSDT_SPOT_COST_V1` are reused through the validated prospective
execution engine. Missing required bars fail closed with explicit data-quality status.

## Safety and adaptation boundary

The observer has only public read-only kline access. It exposes no order route, imports
no exchange order client, requires no credential, and always records real money false.
Its UI is explicitly labelled automated paper research and never presents results as
Champion performance.

ALIGNED parameters, execution geometry, cost model, and the observer contract are frozen
until at least 20 completed automated shadow trades. That boundary permits later
scientific review but does not declare the sample sufficient. Integrity fixes are
versioned and never rewrite prior evidence.

Historical accounting remains 26 completed experiments, 12 observed material historical
hypotheses, zero sealed queries, Champion `NONE`, and real money false. Automated shadow
observations receive separate prospective counters and never count as historical
experiments.

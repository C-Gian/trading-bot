# ADR-0051 — Owner-directed professional trading knowledge-base study

Status: ACCEPTED — Owner direction recorded by Research Director, 2026-09-26

## Trigger

The Owner supplied a curated Google Drive library of professional trading books, textbooks,
academic papers, institutional research and datasets and explicitly directed the Research Director
to study it progressively.

This is a material knowledge/resource input to the project.

## Decision

Authorize a Research-Director-led knowledge programme:

`PROFESSIONAL_TRADING_KNOWLEDGE_BASE_V1`

The purpose is to reconstruct a defensible professional trading knowledge base before designing any
successor trading system.

The programme must:

- inventory every accessible source;
- identify title/authors/year/type from contents when necessary;
- classify source/evidence tier;
- preserve source provenance and rights uncertainty;
- study the corpus progressively;
- distinguish source claims from Research Director interpretation;
- register contradictions rather than force consensus;
- build a topic/relevance map;
- extract permanent research-method rules;
- only later derive a professional signal/decision architecture.

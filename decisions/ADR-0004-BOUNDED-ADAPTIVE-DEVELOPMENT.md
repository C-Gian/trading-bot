# ADR-0004: Cumulative search memory and bounded development

Status: Accepted prospectively for WP-004, before new strategy results.

WP-003 rejected simple trading baselines at default costs. Positive gross breakout
behavior is an observed, unstable clue and motivates an adaptive descendant, not
a reset to an independent first hypothesis. Research memory therefore resolves
behavioral duplicates and root-family ancestry before admission and accumulates
all structural configurations, execution profiles, decisions, and outcomes.

WP-004 allocates one core economic claim and at most three structural variants,
with four fixed profiles each. There is no parameter search or fourth variant.
The committed schedule uses six annual expanding chronological development folds,
a nine-day purge and one-day embargo, exact half-open UTC bounds, no fitting, and
one preselected primary variant. Exposed development history remains exposed.
The primary strategy design memo defines the actual claim and its alternatives.

Use existing engine V2 semantics. New orchestration conservatively withholds entry
through an exit minute whose intrabar ordering is unknown; a future exit cannot
free a position for a decision already taken at that minute's open. Preserve all
WP-003 records and explicitly limit descriptive comparisons with their adapter.
No sealed evaluation, Champion, paper action, new asset, or real capital is authorized.
